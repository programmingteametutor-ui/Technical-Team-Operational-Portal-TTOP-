How incentives are calculated

  • Same client, subsequent round (2→3, 3→4...) → ₹500 to the Tool Drive person
  • Round 1 → Round 2 progression → ₹100 to the Tool Drive person
  • Final round + Placed → ₹2,000 Tool Drive + ₹2,000 Candidate POC (₹4,000 combined if same person)

User Approvals

Anyone who signs in with Google shows up here as pending until an admin approves them. Role/Status can also be edited directly in the "Users" sheet in your Google Sheets workbook.

Data & Backup

Candidates, interviews and incentives are stored in this browser's local storage. All six data sets are also kept in sync with a Google Sheets workbook (see Settings below). Use the tools here to back up or restore this browser's data.

Google Drive & Sheets

🟢 Live – saving to Google Drive & Sheets

Assessment file uploads are saved to Google Drive, and your data is synced with a Google Sheets workbook, through a small Apps Script web app (see apps-script/README.md). The connection is already set up; change it here only if you redeploy the Apps Script.

Workbook sync

Candidates, Assessments, Interviews, Incentives, Candidate POC Team and Support Team are kept in one Google Sheets workbook (one sheet each). Changes made here appear in the workbook within a few seconds; rows you add, edit or delete in the workbook appear here within about 30 seconds — or press Sync now.
